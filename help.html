Operational Training Manual

New Staff Operating Standard

Sunrise Dental Clinic uses a 3-Tier Java Architecture with 7 GoF Design Patterns to prevent double bookings, lost records, long waiting times, and billing errors.

1

User Authentication & Session Security

Only authorized staff members (Receptionists, Dentists, Administrators) can log into the clinic management system.

  • Enter your assigned staff username and password on the Login Page.
  • Demo Staff Credentials for testing: admin / admin123 or receptionist / dental123.
  • The system establishes an encrypted HTTP session cookie to protect patient confidentiality.
2

Registering New Patient Appointments

Follow these exact steps when a new or returning patient calls or arrives at the clinic:

  1. Navigate to the Register New Appointment page.
  2. Fill in patient details: Full Name, Contact Number, and Residential Address.
  3. Select the attending Dentist and Treatment Type (Routine Checkup, Teeth Whitening, Root Canal, Braces, etc.).
  4. Pick the requested date and time slot. The system checks availability to prevent double-bookings.
  5. Check any required clinical add-ons (Digital X-Ray, Local Anaesthesia, Fluoride Treatment, Post-Care Kit). The Decorator Pattern calculates the price dynamically.
  6. Click Confirm & Register Appointment. The system assigns a unique Appointment Number (e.g. APT-1004) and dispatches automated Email & SMS confirmations via the Observer Pattern.
3

Displaying Records & Undoing Accidental Edits (Memento)

Quickly look up appointment details and revert changes if a staff member makes a scheduling mistake:

  • Go to the Patient Details Page and type the appointment number into the search bar.
  • View complete patient contact info, assigned dentist, treatment type, schedule, and cost breakdown.
  • If a schedule edit was made by mistake, click the Undo Last Edit (Memento Pattern) button to immediately restore the appointment snapshot!
4

Calculating & Printing Itemized Patient Bills

Generate accurate itemized receipts and print physical/PDF patient invoices:

  1. Open the Billing Page.
  2. Select the target Appointment Number from the dropdown list.
  3. The system displays the itemized receipt table showing base treatment fee, dentist consultation fee, and individual add-ons.
  4. Click Print Official Receipt to send the invoice directly to the clinic printer or save as a PDF.
5

Safely Exiting the System

Always log off safely when leaving your work desk:

  • Click the red Exit / Logout button in the top navigation bar.
  • Confirming logout will safely destroy active session tokens and return you to the Login screen.
